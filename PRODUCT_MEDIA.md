# Seller images, price, and stock

Seller listings support up to five PNG, JPEG, or WebP images, a nonnegative THB price with at most two decimal places, and a whole-number stock count from zero to 10,000,000. Images are limited to 2 MiB and 16 million pixels each. The backend validates image contents and declared format; remote URLs and SVG are not accepted. The first image is the catalog thumbnail, and customers can switch images in the product gallery.

`POST /v1/products` and version-checked `PUT /v1/products/{id}` save `images` (data URLs), `price` (decimal serialized as a string), and `stock` with the listing and its latest verification in SQLite. Each new report includes a hash of the complete listing snapshot in its audit chain. Earlier reports retain their original values. Existing records default to no images and unspecified price/stock; the seller must fill in price and stock before saving through the form. Product creation and editing also require a non-null price at the API boundary, while legacy verification snapshots remain readable. No database migration is needed.

Customer catalog refreshes show saved changes. Legacy products without prices stay out of the customer catalog until edited and priced. Unspecified stock or zero stock disables adding seller products to the cart. Quantities are capped at available stock and the demo cart's 99-item limit. A later stock reduction adjusts the cart and displays a notice. The cart is local and does not reserve or decrement inventory. Verification eligibility remains independent of stock availability; only qualifying passing products have a clickable badge.

Images are stored inline for this local demo, including local drafts when browser storage permits. Production object storage, authenticated upload ownership, and transaction-backed inventory remain future work. Oversized drafts report a browser-storage failure without publishing changes.

The seller workspace includes “ดูสินค้า / View product” links beside priced saved products and for the listing currently loaded or just saved. They open `/products/{id}` in a separate tab using the stable product ID, so the customer page loads the latest saved images, price, stock and verification result. Unsaved seller edits stay in the seller tab and are not previews of customer-visible content. New unsaved listings and standalone historical verification reports do not get a customer link. Denied or unknown saved products can still be opened; their customer pages follow the existing rule of showing a clickable badge only for qualifying passes.

Seller product summaries show the first saved image (or an empty-image placeholder), a two-decimal THB price, stock, and an explicit APPROVED/PENDING/DENIED/UNKNOWN status. Separate Edit and View product actions prevent status text from acting as the only edit control. A focus refresh updates saved summaries without overwriting an unsaved form; optimistic version checks still reject stale saves. Missing legacy prices show “ต้องระบุราคา” and have no customer link until corrected.

The reviewer workspace also lists saved products with image, price, stock, and the latest persisted verification status. “Open report” retrieves that row’s verification ID and displays its detailed stepper, reasons, and audit trace. The list refreshes on focus, visibility restoration, and every five seconds while visible. List updates preserve an already-open historical report; the reviewer explicitly opens the new result. Failed list requests clear stale summaries and expose a retry action, independently of manual report lookup and session history.

## Matched passing demos

The two legacy LumaVale seller demos are repaired with `python -m scripts.repair_demo_products --base-url http://localhost:8000`. The script only targets their original titles and legacy identity. It reads the existing DEMO-001 identity from the registry adapter and submits corrected names, descriptions and supplement packaging through versioned product updates. Existing price/stock and historical verifications are preserved; reruns are no-ops. Registry fixtures and verification policy are unchanged.

`frontend/assets/demo-supplement.png` is a raster rendering of the existing local `demo-supplement.svg` illustration. It depicts generic supplement packaging, not a real branded product or evidence of authenticity. The passing demo uses Demo Supplement / Demo Brand consistently; real-brand reference photos remain unverified. No automatic photo matching or new reviewer requirement is introduced.
